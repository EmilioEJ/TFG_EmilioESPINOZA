  {Abstract}
  {This Curricular Integration Work addresses the limitations of current institutional document repositories and academic information interfaces, which lack semantic search and proactive interactivity, resulting in delays during routine student inquiries and administrative overload in academic secretariats. Aiming to develop a smart virtual assistant prototype with a 3D avatar interface and computer vision for the dissemination of regulations and curriculum grids, the Design Science Research Methodology (DSRM) was adopted alongside the Agile Scrum framework for iterative software development. The proposed hybrid architecture locally executes visual bounding-box detection and WebGL 3D rendering on the client device, while a local server orchestrates a Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) engine using the paraphrase-multilingual-MiniLM-L12-v2 multilingual embedding model and the local Nemotron model. The main results demonstrate high semantic precision with a 92.0\% Hit Rate in retrieving academic regulations, an average response time (TTFB) of 450 ms that ensures conversational fluency via WebSockets, and a positive usability evaluation among 13 students regarding system integration, accuracy, and user preference. In conclusion, the decentralized architecture and generative artificial intelligence provide a viable and robust solution that transforms traditional static document browsing into an interactive, accessible, and effective support channel for the university community.}
